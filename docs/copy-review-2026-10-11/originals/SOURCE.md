# PigHub postcard images

Eight original images (seven WebP and one JPEG) were obtained from the visible gallery at https://pighub.top/ on 2026-10-09 using the browser's pageAssets bundler. They are copied unchanged under `game/assets/final/pighub/`. Exact image URLs, original titles, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `game/assets/final/pighub/source.json`.

The human project owner explicitly authorized use of PigHub images on 2026-10-05 and again requested PigHub-based reward postcards on 2026-10-09. This is the project's authorization basis, not a claim that the files are user-created, public domain, AI-generated, or that the website proves ownership of every submitted meme. The website's playful footer is not treated as a formal blanket license. Independent authorship/license verification and final release approval remain pending.

The files are packaged for offline access. The running game never requests PigHub, sends mail over a network, collects player information or uploads postcards. Original Chinese text is preserved in the images; localized card titles and letters accompany them. These pictures do not replace the pet's body, eyes or animation artwork. All observed pupils are black; some eyes are closed or have white sclera.

Original titles: 你醒啦, 萝卜猪, 扁猪, 欢乐多猪, 乱停猪, 你知道吗, 笨笨的猪气势汹汹地登场！, 肥猪.

Although gallery URLs end in `.jpg`, seven downloaded responses actually contain WebP, and only `postcard_belly` contains JPEG. Local filenames use the decoded format extension; no image bytes were converted, resampled or edited.
